Policy on the Board Planning Committee

The Board Planning Committee is a standing committee of the board, established under section 2.F. of the bylaws to support the effective and timely functioning of board meetings.

The Board Planning Committee is not a general-purpose decision-making body. It has no authority to decide substantive matters that come before the board, to exercise any of the board's prerogatives under section 2.E. of the bylaws, or to make any decision that requires a board vote. Deciding that a matter should not come before the board via a meeting does not decide the matter itself; it only means the matter will be addressed through some other means (e.g., email, committee action, or administrative action). This authority does not affect any other provision of the bylaws, including the ability of three board members, or the Executive Director acting alone, to call a special meeting under section 2.B.ii.

1. Composition: The Board Planning Committee shall include the Executive Director as a member. The remaining members shall be board members appointed by the board, and shall serve at the pleasure of the board. Any board member, whether or not a member of the committee, may attend meetings of the Board Planning Committee.

2. Responsibilities and Authority: The Board Planning Committee's responsibilities are limited to meeting planning and scheduling, and the board delegates to the Board Planning Committee the authority to carry out the following:
  – set the schedule, time, and location of board meetings within the framework adopted by the board,
  – maintain the board calendar,
  – decide what should come to the board via a meeting and what shouldn't,
  – send the meeting notices required by section 2.B. of the bylaws,
  – set a deadline and process by which board members may submit items for the agenda,
  – compile and distribute the written agenda and any supporting materials in advance of meetings,
  – coordinate logistics for remote participation under section 2.B.iv. of the bylaws,
  – track outstanding action items from prior minutes, described in section 2.C. of the bylaws, and
  – track board member terms, as described in section 2.A. of the bylaws, and notify the board of upcoming term expirations.

3. Decision-making: Within the scope of its delegated authority described in section 2 above, the Board Planning Committee may make decisions in either of the following ways:
  – by unanimous consent of its members, including without holding a meeting of the committee, confirmed in writing (including by e-mail); or
  – by unanimous consent of the board members in attendance at a meeting of the committee, provided that board members were given written notice of the meeting (including by e-mail) at least one week in advance.

The Board Planning Committee may set its own meeting schedule. Because its purpose is to plan board meetings, it may make sense for the committee to meet at least as often as the board meets as a whole.

A meeting of the Board Planning Committee requires the attendance of at least three committee members in order for the committee to make a decision under this section.

Notice of any meeting of the Board Planning Committee shall be given to the full board at the same time it is given to committee members, so that any board member may choose to attend as provided in section 1 above.

4. Accountability: The Board Planning Committee is accountable to the full board and shall keep the board apprised of the board calendar and upcoming meeting plans.
